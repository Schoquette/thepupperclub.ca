Coming Soon · A Sneak Peek

Rescue Trips.

International adventures that pair real volunteer time at a local dog rescue with the kind of slow, intentional travel that lets you actually be somewhere. Surf. Kite. Culture. Sightseeing. And dogs — always dogs.

A hybrid you won’t find anywhere else.

Each Rescue Trip is half meaningful volunteer time at a local rescue — walking, feeding, socialising, helping where it’s needed — and half doing the thing you love wherever we are. Small groups, locally led, with downtime built in.

I want to build not only relationships with rescues, but friendships with like-minded people — experiences that bond you forever. Dog rescue brought out a new, selfless, unconditionally caring and loving side of me. It gave me purpose: bringing the skills and knowledge I had to a cause that needed them.

Partners, dates, and pricing are being finalised now. If something below catches your eye, tap I’m interested and you’ll be first to know.

From Sophie

Where this comes from.

For the past five years I’ve been the Overseas Coordinator at Niagara Dog Rescue, helping bring more than 100 dogs a year into adoptive Canadian homes. (They also do extraordinary work for dogs right here in Canada — the rescue’s heart is local first.)

Alongside that I’ve volunteered on the ground in Mexico, visited shelters throughout Central America and Korea, walked dogs at local rescues, and built relationships with BC-based partners. During Covid I drove and flew across the border more than a dozen times to bring rescues home myself.

The people doing this work day in and day out are some of the most selfless I’ve ever met. They’re chronically under-resourced and often quietly exhausted. Sometimes what they need isn’t more money or supplies — it’s a fresh pair of hands, and reassurance that what they’re doing matters.

I’ve met dozens of people back home who’d jump at the chance to help if there were a thoughtful way in. This is that way in.

How it works.

A simple shape, repeated everywhere we go.

01 — Length

7–10 days

Long enough to make a real dent at the rescue. Short enough to fit into a normal vacation slot.

02 — Cost

Reasonable, no profit

Costs are kept low and transparent. I don’t take a margin on Rescue Trips — the trip pays for itself, not me.

03 — Fundraising

$500+ to the host rescue

Each participant raises a minimum $500 contribution that goes directly to the local rescue partner. In these shelters, that money goes a long way.

04 — Optional add-on

Be a flight angel

If the timing’s right, escort a rescue dog home to their adoptive or foster family in Canada or the US. Thinking about adopting yourself? It’s a wonderful way to “try one on for size”.

Real transport days. Whether you’re escorting one dog as a flight angel or helping a whole van head north, you’re part of the chain that gets them home.

Most trips will only feel like a beginning. The point is to walk away connected — to the rescue, to the people, to a few of the dogs — and to keep showing up however works for your life. That’s exactly what happened to me.

Not sure which one? Tap any “I’m interested” and mention in the comments that you’re open to anything — or that you’d like to be considered as a flight angel — and we’ll keep you in the loop on every trip.
