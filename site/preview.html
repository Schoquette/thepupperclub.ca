What we do

No rushing. No large groups. Just attentive, consistent care.

Good to know

What’s included in every visit?

An enrichment-focused walk, paw wipe and paw balm (the Post-Walk Refresh), fresh water, GPS tracking, feeding and medication if needed, and a full report card with photos.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes — up to 4 weeks per year in one-week increments. No penalty, no stress. Life happens and we plan for it.

How does the subscription work?

After the meet & greet, walks are scheduled on a recurring weekly basis. Billing is monthly — no chasing down bookings, it’s handled.

What areas do you serve?

Port Moody and surrounding areas. Not sure if you’re in range? Reach out and we’ll let you know right away.

Will it always be the same person?

Yes. Sophie is a solo operator and your dog will always see a familiar face. If a replacement is ever needed for a specific day, you will always be notified in advance.

What about reactive or anxious dogs?

The small roster and one-on-one approach is especially well-suited for dogs who do better with routine and a familiar, trusted face.

Start with a free meet & greet.

Before any commitment, we meet you and your dog in person — no charge, no pressure. It’s how we make sure we’re the right fit.
