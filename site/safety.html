Safety & Gear

The thoughtful systems behind every walk, hike, and visit.

Safety is the priority. Full stop.

Your happiness and your pup's peace of mind both matter to us, deeply — but if the two are ever in conflict, safety wins. We're okay disappointing you if that's what it takes to keep your pup safe, and we'll always tell you why. We communicate everything, so you're never left wondering.

Every relationship starts with a thorough meet and greet, so we're aware of any existing medical or behavioural considerations before your pup ever joins a walk or a pack. Nothing about how we care for your dog is left to guesswork.

How we keep every pup safe

Small Group Sizes

Pack hike numbers are kept intentionally small. We never trade a dog's experience — or safety — for a bigger roster.

Temperament Tested

Every dog is evaluated before joining a pack. Energy, size, and temperament are matched thoughtfully, never just by whoever's free that day.

Pet First Aid Certified

All handlers are certified in Pet First Aid by the Red Cross, so we're prepared for the unexpected.

Satellite Communication

Some of our hikes take us just outside cell service. We carry a satellite communicator (inReach), so we're always reachable, wherever the trail goes.

GPS Trackers

Dogs are suited up with AskAVet or Tractive GPS trackers on their off-leash adventures. Not only do the AskAVet trackers provide live and hyper-accurate location in case of emergency, they also provide health data. We highly recommend them for your own pup's everyday use!

Weather-Aware Adjustments

In extreme summer heat, we shorten and adjust walks to protect paws and prevent overheating, and make sure there is water accessible during pack hikes. In winter, we watch for cold, ice, and flooding, and adjust routes and pacing accordingly.

Equipment Maintained

Leashes, harnesses, and safety gear are regularly inspected and maintained — nothing is left to chance.
